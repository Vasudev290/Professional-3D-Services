Our Process

Streamlined 4-Step Rendering Workflow

From initial CAD ingestion to final delivery, our quality-controlled pipeline guarantees rapid execution and precision results.

01

Submit Assets & Brief

Share your 3D CAD files, design blueprints, or reference photos alongside material and lighting specifications.

02

Modeling & Texturing

Our 3D specialists sculpt geometry, apply high-res PBR textures, and set up realistic digital materials.

03

Preview & Revisions

Review draft camera angles and lighting setups. Request tweaks and adjustments before final high-resolution rendering.

04

Final 4K Delivery

Receive high-resolution 4K/8K images, 360 spin files, and optimized AR 3D assets ready for deployment.
